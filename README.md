# velnixnail


A modern online platform designed to connect nail artists with clients through an easy and convenient booking experience.

Velnix Nail helps nail technicians showcase their services, display their portfolios, manage appointments, and build their online presence. Customers can explore nail artists, view their work, compare services, and book appointments with ease.

Features

* Nail artist profiles
* Online appointment booking
* Service and price listings
* Portfolio and gallery showcase
* Artist discovery and search
* Shareable booking links
* User-friendly interface
* Responsive design

About

Velnix Nail is a digital solution created to simplify the nail salon booking process and help nail artists grow their businesses through a professional online presence.

Developed by Velnix Digital Solution.
